We start with sold data, not asking prices — asking prices are opinions, sold prices are facts. Of the four comparable sales, three sit on Rosefield Rd and Memory Lane within a few minutes of your door, and two are in your own building complex. Once we account for what each one has that your home doesn’t (or doesn’t have that your home does) — extra washrooms, a full renovation, more square footage, an end-unit premium — those four sales point to adjusted values between $600,000 and $630,500.

That cluster is what sets the range. The low end reflects the largest, most heavily upgraded comparable in the set once its size premium is backed out; the high end reflects a fully renovated unit even after accounting for its smaller footprint. What’s currently active in Liverpool — including another unit in your own building — is asking between $599,999 and $699,800, which tells us where the competition is positioning itself, though those are asking prices, not results yet.

We are not the market. The buyers are. This range is where the data points us today — we’ll refine it once we’ve walked through your home together and can factor in anything the brokersheets can’t capture.
